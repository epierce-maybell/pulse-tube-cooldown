Test-stand steady-state diagnosis, 2026-11-06

Inputs: data/test_stand/fridge.toml at a9e9d86, its unchanged one-compressor
drive and placeholder head parameters, no applied heater power, boundaries
held at 295 K. Production code and physical inputs were not changed.

Two distinct problems

1. The direct steady-state search from the uniform 295 K initial condition
   enters an inverted-temperature branch and stalls with nonzero heat flow.
   solve_steady_state minimizes squared heat imbalances in log temperature;
   its bounds (2 to 1000 K) do not enforce an ordered regenerator profile.
   A first run capped at 100 function evaluations reached a maximum imbalance
   of 3.526 W. Restarting from that result terminated on xtol after 90 more
   evaluations, at 3.326 W. Stage 1 was 12.851 K, stage 2 was 34.569 K,
   and the hottest second-section matrix node was 264.380 K.
   The residual check correctly rejects this result.

2. A cold equilibrium exists, but the equilibrium found is dynamically
   unstable because of the Gd2O2S heat-capacity feedback in regenerator flow.
   Starting stage 1 at 45 K and stage 2 at 5 K, with each section's matrix
   temperatures linearly interpolated at its node centers between its end
   temperatures, converged in 39 function evaluations (about 15 s).
   Stage 1 = 43.21967056 K; stage 2 = 3.13403475 K.
   Maximum free-node imbalance = 7.45e-11 W, well below 1e-6 W tolerance.

   Equilibrium temperatures in configured graph order, in K:

Focused instability check

At equilibrium, form the free-node heat-flow Jacobian with centered
temperature differences of 1e-4 times each temperature, and divide each row
by that node's total matrix-plus-gas heat capacity. Its dominant eigenvalue
pair is approximately 0.53394 +/- 0.88498j per second. Positive real parts
mean growing perturbations; the growth time is about 1.87 s.

The coldest Gd2O2S node is on the descending side of the specific-heat peak.
Its self-derivative dQ/dT is +5.2135 W/K. Around its equilibrium temperature:

  Temperature (K)   Matrix heat capacity (J/K)   Net heat into node (W)
  5.37051750        1.412849                     -0.055432
  5.38051750        1.365020                      0
  5.39051750        1.321955                     +0.049169

In cooler/head.py:_energy_flow, matrix_capacity enters both the gas/matrix
capacity ratio in pressure_flow and the denominator in regeneration. As
this node warms, its matrix heat capacity falls rapidly, and both terms
increase its net heating. Tube-wall conduction provides only a small
opposing response. This dependence acts in the heat-flow equation itself,
not just in the heat capacity multiplying dT/dt.

Controlled check: freeze only the eight Gd2O2S matrix capacities inside
_energy_flow at their equilibrium values, retaining all other temperature
dependence and the same equilibrium. The residual remains 7.45e-11 W.
The largest real part of the dynamics eigenvalues becomes -0.00239838 /s;
the coldest node's dQ/dT becomes -1.64207 W/K. Difference steps of 1e-4
and 1e-5 give the same conclusion. This isolates the destabilizing feedback
to the Gd2O2S matrix-capacity dependence in the regenerator energy flows.
Freezing capacity was an in-memory diagnostic, not a proposed physical fix.

Follow-up isolation: freezing Gd2O2S capacity only in the regeneration
denominator makes the largest real eigenvalue -0.00281507 /s. Freezing it
only in the pressure-flow and acoustic-power capacity ratios leaves an
unstable eigenvalue with real part +0.104787 /s. The regeneration term is
therefore the first target for a discretization correction. These checks
retain the same equilibrium and all other temperature dependence.

Stage-coupling check at this equilibrium: hold stage 1 and the boundaries
fixed, perturb stage 2, and solve the linearized matrix energy balances.
With heat into a stage positive, dQ1/dT2 = -0.138037 W/K in the original
model. Freezing Gd2O2S capacity only in regeneration changes it to
-0.123623 W/K. Centered differences of 1e-4 and 1e-5 relative temperature
agree. The derivative uses J_stage - J_stage,matrix * inverse(J_matrix) *
J_matrix,stage, so the matrix is re-equilibrated rather than held fixed.
The stability diagnostic therefore does not correct the coupling sign at
this operating point. The proposed integrated-flux discretization has not
been implemented or checked for coupling; these results are not a test of
that proposal, nor of other fitted parameters or operating points.

Implications

A cold initial guess addresses the demonstrated root-finding failure, but
the resulting root cannot be treated as a stable cooldown endpoint.
The next model investigation should focus on _energy_flow's treatment of
the sharp Gd2O2S capacity variation across node boundaries. This check does
not establish whether a different discretization or fitted head parameters
remove the instability, nor whether another stable equilibrium exists.
Increasing optimizer iterations or loosening the residual tolerance does
not address the identified physical feedback. No parameter grid was run.

Coverage gap: test_test_stand_template.py checks construction and fixed
boundaries only. test_steady_state.py checks simple conductive graphs.
The separate bare-head cooldown test uses 1.4 Hz and no shield-radiation
link; the template uses 1.0 Hz with shield radiation.
