Audit of the excessive oscillating flow, 2026-11-06

Conclusion

There was a numerical implementation error, now corrected in the isolated
balanced.py prototype. It exaggerated the cold-end flow but does not explain
the remaining failure. The local first-order gas/matrix temperature expansion
is invalid at the equilibria tested. This does not establish that the broader
pressure/flow network is unusable, or that every operating branch is invalid.
No production physics or recorded model decision changed.

Implementation error and correction

The ideal adiabatic pulse tube has uniform oscillating pressure p. With U=m/rho,
the proposed continuity and energy equations reduce exactly to
  dU/dx = -i*omega*A*kappa_s*p,
where x runs from the cold end to the warm end, A is tube area, and kappa_s
is helium's adiabatic compressibility. Therefore
  U_cold = U_warm + i*omega*B_tube*p,
  B_tube = integral(A*kappa_s dx).
Real-gas kappa_s = kappa_T - beta^2*T/(rho*cp). For ideal gas, B_tube=V/(gamma*P).

The previous 24 equal-temperature cells did not integrate the cold density
change accurately. At T_cold=3.4 K, Pmean=1.565067 MPa:
  exact mass-flow admittance       5.4460e-9 + i*7.5402e-8 kg/(s Pa)
  old cell-based admittance        8.5790e-9 + i*1.1866e-7 kg/(s Pa)
The relative complex error was57.4%; stage1's error was0.14%.

balanced.py now uses the exact volume-flow identity and one scalar quadrature
for B_tube. This removes the tube transfer-cell mesh. Tests independently
check ideal-gas admittance, acoustic-power conservation, and the resulting
end states at cold temperatures3.4,58,and295 K. Before correction two tests
failed and the isothermal case passed; all three pass after correction.
The original check.py/results.json and balanced_results.json are historical
calculations; the corrected audit outputs are separate files.

Why large flow remains

The stage2 tube compliance is7.3438e-11 m^3/Pa. At600 kPa local pressure
amplitude its compression alone draws about.0452 kg/s peak at the cold end.
This flow is predominantly in quadrature with pressure: it compresses and
expands the tube gas, without representing an equivalent mean mass demand
through the warm valve. Cold mass flow is cold density times volume flow.
That exact identity explains the large reactive load independently of the
transfer-matrix implementation. The current model's density-compression-only
inventory estimate lacks the advected-density contribution and gives a tube
reactive mass estimate about18 times smaller at the same mean state. This
comparison is of harmonic compression terms, not unlike waveform conventions.

No sign or unit error was found in the audited continuity/enthalpy equations.
HEPAK expansion_coefficient is beta*T, which the code handles explicitly;
its cp agrees with the production mass-specific accessor. Stored cell end
states agree with their transfer equations to relative error below5e-16.
These checks do not validate all empirical transport closures or discretization.

At the matched-supply point, the worst relative excursion is92.0 K around

approximation at this profile. Stage heat flows remain positive(+2.73,+6.23 W).
Stability and coupling slopes were not calculated from this invalid cycle.

Classification and limits

Implementation: a substantial tube integration error was found and fixed.
Quick equation repair: none established for the remaining large excursion.
The corrected tube equation simplifies the implementation but does not rescue
the operating cycle. The existing supply constraint helps but is insufficient.
Framework: the first-order local thermal closure fails at these tested
operating states. This is narrower than rejecting a pressure/flow network or
the stage energy balances. Other equilibrium branches, fitted physical
parameters, charge feedback and finite-cycle thermal behavior were not resolved
by this audit; none can be claimed as a fix. No additional loss terms or
arbitrary amplitude caps were added.
