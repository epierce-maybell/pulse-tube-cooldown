Human assessment

The user considers the current fit quite good and is not particularly concerned
about its residuals. Further investigation is worthwhile. The earlier assistant
judgment that the fit was poor is superseded by this assessment. Numerical
residuals remain in results.json and fit_report.txt.

Diagnosis of the initial failed fitting procedure

1. Constraining every matrix node between its two plate temperatures excluded
   equilibria permitted by the recorded real-gas equations. A regenerator can
   have an interior temperature below its cold heat exchanger. Snodgrass et al.,
   Cryogenics 132 (2023), Appendix B, report a measured regenerator minimum near
   2.75 K with the cold heat exchanger regulated to 3 K and no applied regenerator
   load: https://tsapps.nist.gov/publication/get_pdf.cfm?pub_id=935876

2. The original outer least-squares fit started log-parameter offsets at zero
   with diff_step=1e-6. SciPy treats this as a relative step and substitutes about
   1.49e-8 at zero. Repeated inner solves, including changing warm starts, produced
   errors large enough to contaminate that finite-difference Jacobian. A measured
   false stage-2 derivative with respect to log(C_1) was about 3.45 W; that derivative
   is exactly zero in these equations because C_1 affects only the plate balances.
   Increasing the perturbation made the false derivative approach zero. The new
   implementation avoids this outer four-parameter finite-difference calculation.

3. An excluded root at the first May point has its coldest matrix node near
   2.885 K while stage 2 is near 3.983 K. Its largest real thermal eigenvalue is
   about +0.131 / s: this particular equilibrium is unstable. Removing temperature
   bounds alone does not select an operating branch. test_fit.py preserves this
   root and the distinction between equilibrium and stability.

4. Profiling the fully nested original calculation placed about 92% of evaluation
   time in solving the drive pressures. Temperature solves nested inside parameter
   finite differences multiplied that expense. The original solid-phase crash was
   not traced to a verified offending state; its precise cause remains unresolved.

5. Small fitted packing constants alone do not establish a model failure. The load-map fit
   explicitly accounts for bypass flow in those constants. A scan that reduces
   a_2 while holding C_2 fixed also changes the relative strengths of passive loss
   and active real-gas transport; failure along that scan is not proof that no fit
   exists.

Structure used by the successful numerical fit

For fixed temperatures and pressures, a stage-2 boundary carries

    F_b = a_2 * D_b + C_2 * E_b,

where F_b is heat flow toward stage 2 at boundary b, D_b is the loss flow per unit
packing constant, and E_b is the real-gas flow per unit valve conductance. Equal
flows at adjacent boundaries give the internal steady-state equations. Dividing
by a_2 leaves the ratio r = C_2 / a_2 as the sole nonlinear head parameter affecting
the steady profiles at fixed valve frequency and drive configuration. The stage-1
internal balances similarly cancel a_1; C_1 enters only the plate balances.

fit.py solves temperatures and the pressure constraints together for each r, then
fits a_1, a_2, and C_1 by nonnegative linear least squares and recovers C_2 = r*a_2.
Every scalar objective evaluation starts from the same branch seeds. Returned
pressures are recomputed with the library drive solver and the resulting physical
column balances are checked. Temperature bounds are the exposed numerical domain
of 2 to 300 K, rather than the plate temperatures. Bounds and seeds used for the
selected ratio search are recorded in inputs.toml and cooling_seed.json.

Numerical results and verification

The latest saved fit takes about 53 seconds. Its coefficients are approximately

    a_1 = 0.1288831663, a_2 = 0.0969125397,
    C_1 = 1.0220023112e-10 W/Pa^2,
    C_2 = 4.2937801495e-11 W/Pa^2.

Stage-1 and stage-2 RMS power residuals are 1.169 W and 0.6417 W. All six matrix
profiles balance to roughly 1e-10 W under independently solved pressures and have
negative largest real thermal eigenvalues with the plate temperatures fixed.

Synthetic parameter recovery passes on the warm and cooling branches. The last
validation comprised four experiment tests, two existing integration tests, and
22 head/drive/measurement tests; all passed. Formatting and lint checks passed.

verification.json records a full-rank local log-parameter Jacobian, condition
number about 119, and relative Jacobian change below 1e-6 between perturbation
sizes 1e-4 and 1e-5. Doubling pulse-tube density quadrature from 48 to 96 points
and resolving the equilibria changes stage powers by at most about 0.000115 W
and matrix temperatures by about 0.0000452 K.

On the sampled cooling branch, fitting stage 2 alone gives about 0.0922 W RMS
at r = 8.196575e-10 and a_2 = 0.02320. The difference from the joint fit makes
stage coupling and shared assumptions useful investigation targets. It does not
identify a specific incorrect physical term or establish a need to replace the
recorded model.

Limits of the findings

The selected fit is a local result on a stable cooling branch. A global minimum
has not been established. Local stability with fixed plates does not establish
reachability from room temperature or agreement with a complete cooldown.

Exploration records cover the warm branch, a cooling branch in both ratio
directions, a colder branch, and sampled hot/cold combinations. Failed continuation
steps are numerical failures, not proofs that an equilibrium does not exist.
Zero-a_2 fits are degenerate: the stage-2 matrix has no thermal restoring rate.
The colder branch and tested positive-parameter mixtures did not improve the
selected result. Raw exploration records describe the implementation at the time
of each search; they are exploratory evidence rather than exhaustive certificates.

The fit uses the May map only, one compressor, 1.0 Hz, gauge-assumed fill pressure,
the paired cooldown's initial TPR as warm-flange temperature, and tube-wall
conduction without MLI radiation. inputs.toml records values and their sources.
The August map and full cooldown predictions have not been tested here. Adding
MLI radiation to a subsequent stand model requires refitting with matching links.

All changes in this investigation were confined to this experiment directory.
The existing library equations and recorded physics were used. The parameter file
is candidate_parameters.toml here; it has not been promoted beside the raw data.
The reusable process for later head models remains deferred, as requested.
